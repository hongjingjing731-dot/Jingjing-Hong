PROJECT 01 /

ELVIS × LEVI'S

MUSIC MARKETING · BRAND PARTNERSHIP · STRATEGY

Developed during my time at Universal Music Publishing, this project explored a potential partnership between Elvis Presley's catalog and Levi's.

The project combined audience and brand research, analysis of Levi's music-led campaigns, Elvis's relationship with fashion and denim, and track recommendations for potential campaign directions.
